The Impact

By shifting from a naive implementation to one that respects the Ampere Architecture's hierarchy, we transformed the workload from being memory-bound to compute-bound. The use of cp.async and L2 Persistence were pivotal in saturating the HBM2e bandwidth and feeding the tensor cores efficiently.
